FAQs

## Questions before you register

The programme terms on the registration page are the final word. These answers summarise them.

Is there a problem statement?

No. The brief is open. Build any working application on Gnani AI's models or APIs that addresses a practical need for enterprises, developers or creators.

Do I need to post my demo before I submit?

Yes. Your submission asks for links to your demo posts, so post first. Nominate one post per platform through your submission. Tagging Gnani AI alone doesn't complete your entry.

What can get an entry disqualified?

Purchased engagement, bots, paid promotion, engagement exchanges, plagiarism and misleading demonstrations are not permitted. Invalid interactions may be excluded, and entries involving substantiated breaches may be disqualified.

How does the stipend work?

Each selected intern receives an individual stipend of up to ₹1 lakh per month. The figure is a ceiling, not a fixed amount, and it is not shared between teammates. Your exact stipend appears in your written offer.

If we apply as a team, are we selected together?

No. Teams of two build and submit one project, but each member is interviewed on their own and considered for an offer separately. One teammate can be selected without the other, so record each person's contribution clearly in your project details.

When and how does the internship run?

Internships run for six months and are scheduled to start from January 1, 2027. Location and working arrangements are confirmed in each written offer.

Does it cost anything to take part?

Every registered participant receives 5,000 programme credits to build with. The terms explain what the credits cover and how usage is handled, and your dashboard shows usage against your credits as you build.

How is my data handled?

Gnani AI uses your registration details to run the programme, assess submissions and contact you about selection. The [**Gnani AI privacy policy**](https://www.gnani.ai/privacy) explains how personal data is processed. Don't put anyone's real phone numbers, account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN or recorded calls into your project or demo. Use synthetic data.

Can I change my project after registering?

Yes. You can update the title, problem, users, models and contributions at any time until you make your final submission.

Can I submit more than one entry?

No. Each participant or team can submit one entry.

Where do I build?

On the Gnani AI API platform at [**app.gnani.ai/voice**](https://app.gnani.ai/voice). Sign in with the email you registered with, then create your API keys and track your credit usage from the dashboard. Docs and quick starts are at [**docs.gnani.ai**](https://docs.gnani.ai/).

I don't have an institutional email, or mine isn't working. What do I do?

We're opening access to non-institutional emails in the second week of October, so you don't need to write in. Once it's live, you can sign in with your personal email.

Can I use the agent builder? Why can't I find API keys for it?

This challenge covers the API platform at [**app.gnani.ai/voice**](https://app.gnani.ai/voice) only. The agent builder sits outside the scope of this internship, and we don't offer APIs for it yet. Your credits work on the models hosted on the API platform.

How do I use Evon?

Evon v3.3 isn't on the API platform yet. It's open weights, so you can request the weights on [**Hugging Face**](https://huggingface.co/gnani/gnani-evon-v3.3-30B-A3B) and run it yourself.

Is there a participant workspace?

No. You describe your project and share your build through the submission form sent to your email. There's no dashboard to manage.

Where do I get help?

Ask build and API questions in the [**Gnani AI Discord community**](https://discord.gg/aECcvtABpq). The [**developer docs**](https://docs.gnani.ai/) cover authentication, streaming and code samples.

Are awards linked to the internship shortlist?

No. Awards are given on the category criteria, irrespective of internship selection.

Can a team or individual win more than one award?

No. Each team or individual can win a maximum of one award, and each award is worth ₹50,000.
